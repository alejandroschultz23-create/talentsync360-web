## 2. Strategic synthesis

### SEO opportunity verdict

**VERDICT: VALIDATED AS A SEARCH OPPORTUNITY; NOT YET VALIDATED AS A CONVERSION OR PRODUCT-MARKET OUTCOME.**

- Google is already associating the existing nearshore URL with the intended commercial cluster.
- The existing URL remains the primary SEO asset. Replacing it or splitting adjacent intent now would discard or dilute the only first-party search signal with meaningful concentration.
- Average position 26 indicates that ranking and intent satisfaction are the primary current constraints. It is an aggregate, not the position of every query; Phase 2.5E must inspect per-query movement and rank-band distribution.
- CTR is a secondary diagnostic, not the primary optimization target. One click makes the 0.2% baseline unstable, and CTR cannot be separated from position or query mix at this stage.
- The current declared title and H1 already align with the dominant commercial intent. Phase 2.5E should not begin with a title-only experiment because that would not address the better-supported gaps: offer completeness, evidence clarity, client responsibilities, and service boundaries.

### Reconciliation of the experiment conflict

D proposed title-only as the narrowest measurable SEO treatment. B and C recommended holding title/H1 constant while changing route-local content architecture. The final decision is to **hold the declared title and H1 constant for the initial Phase 2.5E experiment**.

Reason: a title-only test is narrower, but the evidence does not identify snippet wording as the primary constraint. The page already targets the correct query, while multiple audits independently identify material intent-satisfaction and commercial-clarity gaps. The initial experiment therefore tests one coherent treatment family—content architecture and offer clarity—without adding a title/H1 confounder. A later title test may be authorized only after ranking visibility improves or position-adjusted CTR evidence identifies a snippet problem.

## 5. Differentiation thesis

> TalentSync360 should differentiate through auditable, role-calibrated candidate evidence that makes tradeoffs explicit and materially reduces the client’s remaining evaluation work.

**THESIS VERDICT: SURVIVES WITH CONDITIONS.**

The broad ideas “structured evidence” and “decision-ready” are already partially occupied by Terminal, IT Mates, Revelo, Tecla, and others. The narrower thesis survives because the reviewed market does not publicly expose a complete, neutral, cross-candidate evidence pack that combines requirement-level proof, limitations, fresh constraints, confidence/status, tradeoffs, and a risk-driven interview handoff.

The thesis is operationally valid only if all of the following become true:

1. One approved role calibration and the same must-have definitions are applied to every candidate in a shortlist.
2. Every material conclusion has a source, method, date, reviewer/accountable role, evidence state, and freshness context.
3. Strengths, gaps, contradictions, unknowns, and limitations are mandatory; the pack cannot be only-positive sales collateral.
4. Technical conclusions connect to an artifact, work evidence, direct observation, or clearly labeled self-report/inference.
5. Candidates are comparable on one shared decision surface, with tradeoffs visible rather than hidden inside separate narratives.
6. Compensation/rate, availability, timezone, and engagement constraints are dated and their verification status is explicit.
7. The handoff states what the client still must validate and supplies interview questions tied to unresolved risk.
8. A human owns final inclusion, contradiction handling, and judgment; AI does not imply truth verification or make the final hiring decision.
9. The data, consent, retention, rebranding, and provenance rules are defined, especially for white-label delivery.
10. At least one real, consent-safe redacted artifact and buyer evidence demonstrate that the pack reduces repeated screening or improves decision clarity.

This is a product and operating standard, not a moat claim. A moat would require demonstrated adoption, consistency, workflow integration, data/governance advantages, and outcome evidence over time.

## 6. TalentSync360 Evidence Pack v1 — OWNER GATE 1

**OWNER DECISION — CLOSED: APPROVED.**

TalentSync360’s approved operating model is: role requirement → semantic AI-assisted talent discovery → professional evidence review → candidate preselection → role-specific technical challenge → technical defense with TalentSync360, maximum 30 minutes → structured candidate evidence → comparable shortlist → client decision support.

TalentSync360 does not define quality by CV keyword overlap alone. AI assists discovery, semantic matching, evidence normalization, and prioritization. AI does not independently validate candidate quality. Human review and role-specific technical validation determine shortlist inclusion.

### Required search/role layer

- Role calibration.
- Business and technical context where applicable.
- Must-have and nice-to-have requirements.
- Seniority expectations.
- Validation criteria.
- Relevant engagement constraints.

### Required candidate layer

- Candidate snapshot and relevant professional evidence.
- Requirement-by-requirement evidence matrix and evidence source where available.
- Role-specific technical challenge, validation method, validation date, and technical result.
- Technical defense of no more than 30 minutes, including observed technical reasoning and tradeoffs.
- Communication evidence observed during the process and seniority evidence where supportable.
- Strengths, gaps, contradictions where present, and unknowns.
- Availability when validated; compensation/rate expectation when applicable and validated; timezone/engagement constraint when relevant.
- Evidence state and confidence where meaningful.
- Recommended client interview questions.

### Required shortlist layer

- Comparable candidate view and material tradeoffs.
- Unresolved risks.
- What TalentSync360 has already validated and what remains for the client to validate.
- Candidate-specific interview focus.

### Optional / evidence-dependent fields

GitHub, portfolio, public project evidence, code sample, candidate video, references, and additional assessment may be included when relevant. They are not mandatory for every candidate. Absence of a public GitHub profile must not automatically penalize a candidate.

### Approved internal product definition

TalentSync360 transforms a technical requirement into a comparable shortlist supported by professional evidence, role-specific technical validation and a technical defense of up to 30 minutes. Each profile distinguishes what is supported, observed, self-reported, unresolved or unknown and shows the client what remains to validate before making the final hiring decision.

This is an approved internal product definition, not necessarily final public copy. Prior evidence limits remain: approval defines the product contract but does not by itself prove consistent real-world delivery, adoption, or buyer outcomes.

## 7. Evidence states

**OWNER DECISION — CLOSED: APPROVED.**

| State | Conservative meaning |
|---|---|
| **VERIFIED** | Evidence or fact checked through a defined source or procedure. |
| **OBSERVED** | Behavior or capability directly observed during the TalentSync360 process. |
| **SELF-REPORTED** | Information supplied by the candidate that has not been independently corroborated. |
| **INFERRED** | Reasoned conclusion derived from available evidence but not directly observed or verified. |
| **CONTRADICTORY** | Material sources or observations conflict. |
| **UNKNOWN** | Insufficient evidence exists to support a conclusion. |

Use these states conservatively. They improve provenance, claim safety, accountability, and comparison but do not imply automated truth verification. AI may assist extraction or normalization; a human owns material state assignment, contradiction handling, and shortlist inclusion.

If confidence is used, it must be **HIGH**, **MEDIUM**, or **LOW** and tied to a specific conclusion or requirement. Unexplained pseudo-precise global AI scores such as “94% match” are not evidence of candidate quality.

**EVIDENCE STATES: APPROVED.**

**DECISION: Evidence Pack v1 exposes reviewer role, review date, rubric/version, validation method, and final inclusion decision.**

| Field | Requirement | Strategic reason |
|---|---|---|
| Reviewer role | Required; named reviewer identity subject to owner/privacy policy | Lets the buyer evaluate whether the reviewer is qualified for the judgment. |

Human accountability is strategically important because “human-vetted” is otherwise commodity language. The differentiating value is not the presence of a person; it is traceable ownership of a bounded judgment under a versioned standard.

## 9. Service boundaries — OWNER GATE 2

**OWNER DECISION — CLOSED: APPROVED.**

### Core product — Shortlist Sprint

The **Shortlist Sprint** is the core product. It includes role calibration, semantic talent discovery, AI-assisted matching/prioritization, professional evidence review, candidate preselection, a role-specific technical challenge, a technical defense up to 30 minutes, a structured Evidence Pack, a comparable shortlist, and a client interview plan.

TalentSync360 controls the quality of sourcing, evidence, validation, and decision support. The client controls the final hiring decision and the employment or contractual relationship.

### Supporting capabilities

- Interview coordination: **SUPPORTED / OPTIONAL**.
- Feedback loop after client interviews: **SUPPORTED**.
- Offer-stage coordination: **LIMITED SUPPORT**, including candidate communication, availability reconfirmation, compensation-expectation reconfirmation, and start-date coordination.

Limited offer-stage support does not mean TalentSync360 acts as legal employer or negotiates legal or employment obligations on the client’s behalf.

### Separate active extended service — Full-Cycle Search

**Full-Cycle Search** is approved as an extended service separate from the Shortlist Sprint. It may include the Shortlist Sprint foundation, candidate communication, interview scheduling, feedback management, pipeline coordination, selection support, and limited offer-stage coordination. The employment/legal relationship remains outside TalentSync360 unless a future separately approved operating model changes it. Full-Cycle Search must not be merged into the Shortlist Sprint definition.

### Separate active / early-stage B2B offer — White-label Talent Intelligence

**White-label Talent Intelligence** is approved for IT consultancies, software factories, staffing firms, and recruiting agencies. TalentSync360 may operate behind the partner workflow as the role calibration → semantic discovery → evidence review → technical validation → Evidence Pack → comparable shortlist layer.

Delivery may be TalentSync360-branded, co-branded, or partner-branded/white-label, subject to governance. White-label refers to the intelligence, evidence, and presentation layer. It does not mean TalentSync360 supplies invisible employees or becomes the legal employer. Internal provenance and reviewer accountability must remain preserved regardless of external branding.

Partner delivery must preserve the partner client relationship/ownership, confidentiality, attribution rules, internal evidence provenance, candidate transparency, and appropriate consent.

### Current out of scope

EOR, payroll, employment compliance services, contractor management, managed staff augmentation, managed engineering teams, software project delivery, guaranteed hiring outcomes, guaranteed employee performance, and guaranteed retention are out of scope.

**OWNER GATE 2: CLOSED — APPROVED.**
